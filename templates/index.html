About the Project

FitForm AI is a beginner college project that uses a TensorFlow/Keras image classification model to categorise a squat photo into one of three classes: Good, Bad Back, or Bad Heel.

The model was trained on a limited dataset. Its current test accuracy is approximately 37.67%, which means it is not reliable and will frequently produce incorrect predictions. All feedback shown is generic guidance and does not reflect a professional assessment of your posture, spine, or injury risk.
